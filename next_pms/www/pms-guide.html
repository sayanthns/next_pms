📘 Next PMS — User Guide

Project management, time tracking, weekly planning and team performance for Enfono. This guide covers everything from your first check-in to how management reads the Performance Score.

App: next_pms · Frappe v15 · Access: /next-pms

Overview

Next PMS is Enfono's project management system: sprint-based IT project tracking with a built-in timer, attendance check-in, weekly capacity planning, meeting calendar, client portal and automated reporting.

Getting Started

  1. Log in at https://office.enfono.com/next-pms with your ERPNext account. No PMS menu? Ask an admin to enable your PMS Access (Settings → Team → User Management).
  2. Check in — click ● Check In at the bottom of the sidebar when you start your day. Check out when you leave. This is your attendance record.
  3. Open My Tasks — everything assigned to you, sorted by priority and due date.
  4. Start the timer on the task you're working on (or press T). The timer is the only thing that counts as logged work hours.
  5. Stop the timer when you switch tasks or take a break. Move the task across the Kanban board as it progresses.
⚠️ Attendance ≠ work hours. Check-in proves you were present; the timer proves what you worked on. Being checked in all day with no timer running = 0 logged hours in every report.

Projects & Sprints

Project lifecycle

Draft→ Active→ On Hold→ Completed

A project holds: budget (mandatory), hourly-rated team members, sprints, tasks, expenses and client-portal settings. Budget utilisation is tracked automatically from timer cost — alerts fire at 80%.

Sprints

Planning→ Active→ Completed

Plan the backlog by dragging tasks into a sprint, activate it, and track burn on the project dashboard and Gantt view.

Time Tracking

  1. Open the task → click Start Timer (or press T).
  2. Work. The elapsed time survives page reloads. A reminder fires if a timer runs 4+ hours.
  3. Stop when done or switching. Duration and cost (hours × your hourly rate) save automatically as a PMS Time Log.

Review or fix your entries under Time Logs. Every report — daily AI report, weekly email, Productivity, Performance — reads from these entries.

⚠️ Forgot the timer? Log time the same day where possible. Reports window hours by the time-log date; late back-filling distorts your daily consistency metric.

Check-In / Check-Out

  • Check in when you start your day, check out when you finish — sidebar button or the mobile app.
  • Missed check-ins/check-outs appear in your weekly email and trigger reminders.
  • Attendance counts only effective working days: Sundays, public holidays (your holiday list) and approved leave are excluded automatically.

Weekly Plan

Management publishes an Excel-style projects × people matrix every week: planned hours per (project, member), plus priorities, closures and a watch list.

  • Team members: check Monday morning — it is your commitment for the week. Hours you log on planned projects feed the Plan Adherence dimension of the Performance Score.
  • Managers: allocations automatically sync each project's planned hours and team; publishing makes the plan visible and scoreable.

Calendar & Meetings

  • Schedule meetings with attendees from the Calendar view; cards are colour-coded by timing.
  • Minutes of Meeting (MoM) are mandatory — a meeting isn't complete until minutes are recorded.
  • Meetings can spawn tasks (source meeting is linked on the task).

Metrics Explained — Utilization vs Efficiency

Two percentages appear across Next PMS and are not comparable to each other:

UtilizationEfficiency
Formulalogged hours ÷ target hours × 100estimated hours ÷ actual hours × 100
Question"Did I log enough hours against the 8h/day bar?""Were the task estimates accurate?" (>100% = faster than estimated)
Low value meansUnder-logged — low activity or timers not runSlower than estimated (or tight estimates)

Target hours — the fair bar

Target = 8h × effective working days. Sundays, public holidays and approved leave are removed before any percentage is computed; half-day leave deducts 0.5 day. You are never penalised for approved absence — a sick day lowers your target, not your score.

Why the weekly email and Task Report can disagree

  • Different windows: the email always covers the fixed Mon–Fri week; Task Report periods roll back from today. Opened on Saturday, a "5d" view covers Tue–Sat — different days summed.
  • Different headline metrics: the email leads with Utilization, the report leads with Efficiency. 49% utilization and 79% efficiency for the same person in the same week can both be correct — volume vs accuracy.
  • Same engine underneath: identical leave/holiday/target logic everywhere; only the window and the question differ.

Performance Score (Management)

A single 0–100 composite used as an input to appraisals, increments and promotions. Weighted across 8 dimensions:

DimensionWeightMeasures
Delivery25%Estimated hours of completed tasks vs target — output volume, size-weighted
Timeliness15%On-time completion of due-dated tasks
Utilization15%Logged hours vs leave-adjusted target
Plan Adherence15%Hours on Weekly-Plan projects vs planned
Efficiency10%Estimate accuracy (capped 120%)
Quality10%1 − task reopen rate
Consistency5%Steady daily logging vs binge logging
Attendance5%Check-in presence on working days

Bands: A ≥ 85 B ≥ 70 C ≥ 50 D < 50. Dimensions with no data are excluded and weights renormalised — missing data never scores zero.

✅ Fairness built in: leave-adjusted everywhere · PM-controlled estimates · anti-gaming caps · evaluate 30–90-day trends, never a single week · metrics inform, humans decide. Full methodology with formulas is published inside the Performance tab itself.

Client Portal

  • Customers get a token link (/pms-portal?token=…) — no ERPNext login.
  • They see progress, sprint status and task lists; they can comment on tasks. Internal costs and budgets are never exposed.
  • PMs manage portal access from the project's Client Settings.

Support Tickets

Client-raised or internal tickets tracked under Support Tickets (managers/admins). Tickets can be converted into PMS Tasks (task type Support Ticket) so the work is timed and reported like everything else.

FAQ

QuestionAnswer
My utilization looks low but I worked all week?Timer discipline — check-in hours don't count as work hours. Run the timer on tasks.
I was on approved leave — does it hurt my numbers?No. Leave days are removed from your target before any % is computed.
Email says 49% but Task Report says 79% — bug?No — two different metrics over two different windows. See Metrics Explained.
Who can see my Performance Score?Management only (System Manager / PMS Manager). Not visible to peers.
Task estimate looks wrong — can I change it?Ask your PM. Estimates are PM-controlled because they feed evaluation metrics.
I can't see Reports / Settings in the sidebarRole defaults. An admin can enable them per user in User Detail → Permissions.
